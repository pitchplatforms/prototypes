P2P-28 · Lane A · Foundations

Proving what was agreed, and when

P2P has no wet signatures. A tick-box is the execution — so the record of it has to be exact: who agreed, to which wording, on whose behalf, at what moment.

Three moments

Where consent is captured

screens belong to other lanes

This ticket builds none of these screens — it builds the consent types they record against, so that when the issuer and investor lanes arrive there is something official to write to. If a consent is missing from this list, it is missing at launch.

Reuse first — every moment already has a screen None of these are new screens. Each moment is an existing journey getting a tweak, named on each card below. The invest screen in particular already carries inline T&C checkboxes — that is precisely the mechanism the note acknowledgments and the click-through execution extend. Building anything parallel here would contradict the launch strategy (ADR-0024, maximum reuse, no parallel journey).
Moment 1 · §1.1

Issuer applies

Four consents, at application submission
  • Consent to credit checks
  • Platform Terms & Conditions
  • PDPA & privacy notice
  • Data sharing with partners where relevant
Recorded against user + company + application — a credit-check consent is about a specific company at a specific time.
Reuse: the RaiseFund application step engine + DynamicForm (pages/raise/application-form) — add consent steps, don't build a journey.
Moment 2 · §2.3

Investor invests

Three acknowledgments, on every investment
  • Risk disclaimer total capital loss · illiquid until maturity · not covered by the Capital Market Compensation Fund
  • Investor category still accurate
  • Disclosure & risk rating reviewed
Recorded against user + note. Repeated per investment — not once per account.
Reuse: tweak the existing invest screen (campaign-details/invest-now/) — it already has inline T&C checkboxes; make them per-product and first-class consents.
Moment 3 · §2.3 / §1.6 / §1.3

The instrument executes

One shared consent type, three uses
  • Investor executes the digital note at payment completion
  • Issuer executes the final Note Supplement at close
  • Issuer executes the Master Issuer Agreement at admission
Built once here as a type; the shared click-through component is F1.4 / F2.3. The note register's ConsentId points at this row.
Reuse: generalise the same invest-now checkbox pattern into the shared component — no e-sign vendor, no new ceremony.

Consolidation

Two pages, not six

The direction is one document per purpose, with per-instrument sections inside — not a separate T&C and risk page for every product. P2P adds sections; it does not add pages.

Versioning

When everyone has to re-accept

A version exists only for a material change — editorial fixes don't create one. And when a version is published, every user re-accepts at their next login, not just P2P users, because part of the amendment (withdrawal limits) reaches anyone with a wallet.

Change typeNew version?Who re-acceptsExample
MaterialYesEveryone, at next loginAdding nominee authority; changing withdrawal limits
EditorialNoNobodyTypo, formatting, citation update
Consequence to handle in the build Because editorial edits don't bump the version, the published page can drift from the exact text a user accepted. Mitigation: keep a lightweight content changelog on the page itself (date + what changed), so the audit story is complete even where no consent version was created. Worth confirming the approach with Legal.
Settled

Decisions this build follows

  • The consent inventory above is complete — four issuer, three investor, one execution, plus platform T&C and PDPA. Anything missing here is missing at launch.
  • Issuer consents record user + company + application, so "who consented to the credit check on this deal?" is answerable years later.
  • Consolidation-first pages: one platform T&C and one risk-warning page, each with per-instrument sections. No standalone P2P pages.
  • Only material changes create a version. Editorial edits are published without one.
  • A new version means everyone re-accepts at next login — including ECF-only users, because the withdrawal-limits clause applies to their wallet.
  • Pages ship dark with draft copy behind notesEnabled; Legal's real text (F-07, PM-03) is a launch gate.
  • The ledger is append-only — a consent row is never updated or deleted, only superseded by a later one.
