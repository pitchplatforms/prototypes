The rule

Live or snapshot

The single most important thing to get right, because it decides whether editing a number quietly rewrites deals that were already struck. Every parameter is one or the other, and the screen it lives on tells you which.

Reads live

Platform fees & statutory rates

What pitchIN charges for a service, at the moment it performs it — plus tax rates and investor limits. A change applies everywhere immediately, including on live notes.

Justified because the management fee is charged when a repayment is received, so the rate in force at that moment is the correct one. Same for wallet fees, SST and limits.
Snapshot onto the note

Deal terms

What the issuer and investors agreed for this specific note — rate, grade, tenure, and the fee and default terms disclosed with it. Copied onto the note when it is created and fixed for its life.

A later config change starts applying to new notes only. Nothing can retroactively alter a deal that was already disclosed and executed.
What this means for the note model Interest rate, risk grade and tenure are already stored on the note itself (P2P-26). The snapshot rule adds a further set of values that must be copied onto the note at campaign setup: success fee · early-settlement fee · renewal fee · late flat fee · late interest rate · grace period · delinquency DPD · default DPD threshold. The credit matrix and product caps need no snapshot — their effect is already captured in the rate, tenure and grade the note stores.

§4.6

Who may approve a change

A committee process, not a software feature — the platform does not enforce this in-app at launch. Editing is restricted to a senior-admin role and the audit trail proves what happened; the approval itself lives in IC and Board minutes.

Material change
Changes to the policy's original intent, to default thresholds, to escalation triggers or authority bands, or anything affecting the Recovery Distribution Waterfall.

Decisions this build follows

  • Platform fees and statutory rates read live; deal terms snapshot onto the note at campaign setup. A config change never rewrites a deal already disclosed.
  • Investor limits are now known — retail RM50,000, angel and sophisticated unrestricted (SC RM 14.32–14.33). No longer a blocked launch gate; Compliance confirms rather than supplies.
  • Retail RM50,000 is a hard block, measured on outstanding exposure at any point in time — headroom returns as notes repay. Consistent with our submitted rulebook §2.3 ("imposed programmatically").
  • Changes take effect immediately — no future-dating. ⚠️ Ops consequence: the stamp-duty exemption ends 31 December 2026 and must be updated by hand on the day.
  • A written reason is mandatory on every change, one rule for all parameters, alongside actor, timestamp and before → after.
  • Editing is restricted to a senior-admin role. No in-app maker-checker at launch — §4.6 approval stays a committee process.
  • Sets can grow. New products and grades can be added through config (App C: its product list "is not exhaustive"); existing entries can be disabled but never deleted, because live notes reference them forever.
Two things for Compliance to confirm, not blockers 1. Para 14.33 says operators must "encourage" retail to stay under RM50,000, while our submitted §2.3 says limits are "imposed programmatically". We are building the stricter reading (hard block) — confirm that is the intended posture. 2. 14.33 caps investment "on any P2P platform", so the RM50,000 counts P2P exposure only and is not aggregated with ECF or PSTX limits, which are a separate regime. We can only see exposure on pitchIN.
