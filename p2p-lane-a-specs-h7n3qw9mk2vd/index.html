The Note — data model & register

What a note is in the database: the terms satellite, the register of who holds which slice, and the versioned repayment schedule. Change the product class or repayment frequency and watch which fields the system derives for itself. Carries the end-state ER diagram across all three instruments.

The Dials — config & parameters engine

Roughly fifty rulebook figures, organised by the admin screen they live on and who owns them. The decisive rule: platform fees read live, deal terms snapshot onto the note. Edit any value in the mock — it insists on a written reason, then writes the audit entry.

The Fine Print — consents & legal pages

P2P has no wet signatures, so a tick-box is the execution and the record of it has to be exact. Run a scenario and watch the ledger rows it writes. Also covers page consolidation and the rule that a material change makes everyone re-accept.

The shelf — product-line scaffolding

One feature flag, one enum member, one route skeleton and the words for it in three languages. Throw the switch and watch every route verdict change with it. Adds no navigation — that is a separate Design workstream.
