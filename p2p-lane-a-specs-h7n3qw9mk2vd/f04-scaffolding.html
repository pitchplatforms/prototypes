What "add a flag" actually costs

It is six files across three repositories, and the flag's source of truth is a CMS checkbox — which is what makes it flippable on launch day with no release.

01 pitchin-knowledge apps/cms/src/globals.ts New checkbox on the feature-flags global. Source of truth; staff-editable; public read.
02 pitchinAPI Services/FeatureFlagsCmsClient.cs Hardcoded to one flag today — GetKnowledgeCentreEnabledAsync probes a single key. Generalise it to read the whole global once.
03 pitchinAPI Models/FeatureFlags/FeatureFlagsDto.cs Response shape returned to the SPA.
04 pitchinAPI Models/FeatureFlags/FeatureFlagSettings.cs Appsettings fallback, used when the CMS read degrades. Default false.
05 pitchinAPI Services/FeatureFlagsService.cs Precedence: CMS wins, appsettings is the safe fallback, never throws.
06 PitchinCustomerWeb core/services/feature-flag.service.ts Read once at boot by APP_INITIALIZER. Defaults false on any error.
Before you start

pitchin-knowledge is not in repos.manifest. It will not be on your machine after a standard bootstrap, and nothing in the P2P docs mentions it. Clone it before you begin, or you will hit a wall with no obvious cause.

Needs a compliance eye

menu.noteDescription is marketing copy for a debt product. It states the mechanism — fixed repayment terms — and deliberately implies no guaranteed return. Any rewording should keep that property.

What needs to be built

  • Flag chain — the six hops above, off by default in every environment including production.
  • Enum — InvestmentType.Note = 2, Token = 3.
  • Routes — /note and /note/:slug with a placeholder page component, behind the canMatch guard. Unlinked and noindex while the flag is off.
  • i18n — the key family above across en-US, ms-MY, zh-CN. Bahasa Melayu and Chinese ship best-effort and are finished by the copy editors through the in-context translation editor (ENB-71) rather than blocking this build.
  • Placeholder page — built on @pitchin/ui. The library is now merged to origin/staging (128 files) and signed off 2026-08-08; this is the first new P2P screen and sets the precedent for lanes B–G.
  • Playwright smoke (protected path, ADR-0009) — flag off: /note resolves, carries noindex, and nothing links to it; flag on: the route is live and indexable. Nav is identical in both states.

Why it resolves instead of 404ing

Dry Run A (P2P-56) is the public-launch gate and is a full raise cycle. Feature flags are global with no per-user or cohort targeting, so a 404 behind the flag would force a choice between skipping the dry run on production and exposing notes to everyone to perform it. An unlinked, noindex route makes the gate passable with neither compromise.

Out of scope. Real listing behaviour (F2.1), the one-pager (F2.2), landing and disclosure pages (F2.7) — all lane D. The consolidated Invest navigation, the navbar redesign and the /invest/:slug URL space belong to Design → Landing Page Redesign. Token consolidation is planned separately and is not P2P work.
