Customise band points

Each band gives 0 to 100 points before the weights apply. Change any value to test a different scale; the defaults are scorecard v0.

Customise weights

Each input’s multiplier is its pillar weight × its share of the pillar × 10. With the defaults, DSCR is 45% × 70% × 10 = 3.15 points for every band point.

Scorecard v0 by default. Bands, PD anchors and the 60% loss given default are placeholders under test, not pitchIN policy. Scores round to the nearest whole number, with exact halves rounding to the even number, to match the test documents.
