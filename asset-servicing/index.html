What we built

Three pillars, and the features inside them

Everything we do for a company after the raise, grouped into three simple jobs: Manage → Communicate → Create value.

Pillar 1

Manage

Keep your shareholder records accurate and in one place.

Finalising a completed raise Live

When a round closes, settle exactly who gets how many shares.

  • Confirm holdings: lock in each investor and how many shares they own
  • Settle fees: confirm and settle pitchIN's fee
  • Notify investors: automatically tell everyone what they now own
Digital shareholder registry Live

One trusted list of who owns what — your single source of truth.

  • One-click import: pull in everyone from a finished campaign instantly
  • New funding rounds: add each new round's shareholders to the list
  • Onboard any company: bulk-add existing shareholders too — even those without a pitchIN account, including companies that never raised with us
Pillar 2

Communicate

Stay connected to your investors — for years.

Investor updates & relations Live

Keep investors informed from one professional place.

  • Company page: a managed profile investors can view, with the original campaign page kept as a reference
  • Announcements: post updates that reach every investor at once
  • Document sharing: send reports and documents securely
Pillar 3

Create value

Give investors a real way to see returns.

Buy & sell shares (our exchange) Live

Our regulated marketplace lets investors trade their private shares.

  • Place orders: create and manage buy & sell orders
  • Auto-matching: buyers and sellers paired automatically, with fees handled for you
  • Built-in wallet: add money and withdraw what you earn
  • Public showcase page: a public page that highlights the company
  • Open to all companies: any private company can use the marketplace, not just crowdfunding ones coming soon
Private share sale Live

Move shares between people directly, with pitchIN handling the paperwork.

  • Direct transfers: move shares between holders, in any direction
  • Record the price: log the agreed price so investors see their shares' real value
Corporate actions Coming soon

Run company-wide actions and pay returns through the platform.

  • Dividend payout: distribute dividends to every shareholder done by hand today
  • Corporate action proposal: let investors collectively decide on a proposed corporate action coming soon
What this means for our team There is now a whole post-raise journey to run — finalising raises, keeping the registry up to date, investor updates, and handling share trades and transfers. Be clear with customers on what's ready: buying and selling shares is live today; opening the marketplace to all companies and automated dividend payouts are on the way.
