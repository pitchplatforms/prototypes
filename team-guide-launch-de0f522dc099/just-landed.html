Get the update

One command, one time.

This update is the one that installs the polite updater — so this first pull is by hand. From then on, the workspace offers updates itself and waits for your yes.

1

Open a window into your folder

Mac: press ⌘-space, type Terminal, press Enter. Type cd  (with a space), drag your ai-setup folder into the window, press Enter.
Windows: open your ai-setup folder in File Explorer, click the address bar, type cmd, press Enter.

2

Type the magic words

Type git pull and press Enter. A few lines scroll by — that’s the update arriving. When it stops, you’re done.

3

Close it and carry on

Close the window and open Claude as usual. Everything on this page is now in your workspace — and CHANGELOG.md keeps the plain-language story.

Saw an error, or it asked you a question? Stop there — you haven’t broken anything, and nothing was changed. Screenshot it, post it in the rollout channel, and we’ll sort it together. Never type anything with force in it.
This is the last manual update. From now on, when an update exists, the workspace tells you at the start of a session and shows what’s inside. Nothing applies until you say yes.
It’s one pull, not five. The update is all-or-nothing by design — that keeps every copy identical. What you adopt is à la carte: take what you want.
